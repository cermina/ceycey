Privacy Policy

01 — What We Collect

ASTRID ATS collects only the minimum personal data necessary to provide our service:

  1. Name and email address (for account creation and subscription management)
  2. Payment information (processed securely by Paddle — we never store card details)
  3. Usage data (anonymous protocol queries for service improvement)
02 — How We Use Your Data
  1. To provide and maintain your subscription access
  2. To send service-related emails (receipts, updates)
  3. To improve our protocol database and user experience
  4. We never sell your data to third parties
03 — Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. Payment processing is handled by Paddle (paddle.com), which is PCI-DSS compliant. We do not store credit card or banking information.

04 — Cookies

ASTRID ATS uses only essential cookies required for the service to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

05 — Your Rights
  1. You may request access to your personal data at any time
  2. You may request deletion of your account and data
  3. You may unsubscribe from emails at any time
  4. Contact: privacy@astridats.com
06 — KVKK (Turkey)

For users in Turkey, your data is processed in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698). You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data.
